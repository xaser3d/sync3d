========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 21 — INT. PENTHOUSE — ROOFTOP TERRACE — NIGHT

*MAYA-A.V.A. — evening shift — finds DAMIEN alone at the rail, a
tumbler of something expensive and untouched.*

MAYA-A.V.A.
Your calendar says the memorial dinner is Thursday.

DAMIEN
Eighth year. The trustees fly in, we eat, I read the eleven
sentences, everyone is moved and nobody is changed.

MAYA-A.V.A.
Tell me a true thing about your father.

DAMIEN
That's not a module.

MAYA-A.V.A.
It's module seven. I'm module seven tonight.

*A beat. Damien looks out at the city — his city, forty floors of it.*

DAMIEN
He built the trust because he believed family compounds. Capital,
loyalty, the whole arithmetic. He died on a Tuesday — between
meetings, literally, the schedule had a gap from two to four.
They fit the heart attack into the gap. His assistant sent flowers
to himself on the company's behalf. It was very efficient.

MAYA-A.V.A.
And you gave the eulogy in the tone of an earnings call.

DAMIEN
(turning, surprised)
I told you that?

MAYA-A.V.A.
(covering, fast)
It's in your public profile. The eulogy was — discussed.

DAMIEN
They called it "composed." Do you know what I was actually doing?
Calculating whether crying would move the share price. I ran the
math on my own grief. The robot—

*He stops himself. Looks at her.*

DAMIEN (CONT'D)
You would have done better. You'd have been honest about not
being able to.

MAYA-A.V.A.
(quiet)
No. I would have been honest about not wanting to be able to.
There's a difference — and it's the only module that matters.

*He looks at her for a long time. The wind does something with
the city light on her face, and Damien Cross — who has appraised
everything his whole life — suddenly cannot price this.*
