## SCENE 20 — EXT. CROSS TOWER — GARAGE — DAY

*DAMIEN and JIM walk toward the car — A.V.A. gliding behind. A.V.A.
suddenly stops. Her head tilts, processing.*

A.V.A.
Do not enter the vehicle.

DAMIEN
It's my car.

A.V.A.
The brake line exhibits a fracture pattern inconsistent with wear.
The cut is clean, recent — within four hours, based on oxidation.
Estimated structural tolerance at speed: negligible.

*Jim crouches, looks under the car. Goes white.*

JIM
There's... something dripping.

*Police tape, a mechanic, photos — LATER. A DETECTIVE questions
Damien while A.V.A. stands at parade rest.*

DETECTIVE
And your — companion — just knew?

DAMIEN
She estimated the structural tolerance.

DETECTIVE
Of a car she never saw before. From standing next to it.

DAMIEN
(shrugging, perfectly sincere)
They build them strong.

*The detective writes it down, unsure what else to do. JIM stares
at Damien — the man who audits everything, defending a miracle to
himself.*
