========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 29 — INT. HOTEL BAR — NIGHT

*SOPHIA VANE — off-duty, no practice smile — meets MAYA (as herself,
hoodie, nobody's robot). Two women who were supposed to be rivals
and are instead co-defendants.*

SOPHIA
You're the girl from the lab. The engineer. Why am I here?

MAYA
Because Victor owns the paper on your family's estate and he's
calling it in. And because last week you walked out of a dinner
rather than sell a yacht story you'd been told was a secret.
That took something.

SOPHIA
(long look)
It took three martinis. What do you want?

MAYA
Victor is going to re-activate you — press, a manufactured romance,
probably a proposal staged at the gala. He'll threaten the estate.
I can get the loan assignment — my lab has friends in places that
surprise people. You don't have to play the bride he wrote.

SOPHIA
Why would you help me? I'm the competition.

MAYA
You're the contingency plan. So am I, in my way. I don't like
being managed either.

*A beat. Sophia studies her — really studies her.*

SOPHIA
You're the one on the staff feed, aren't you. The evening one.
He laughs with you — I've seen the gala photos. You're not the
robot.

MAYA
(very still)
I'm the engineer.

SOPHIA
You're the evening one.

*A silence that isn't a denial. Sophia smiles — her first real
smile of the film.*

SOPHIA (CONT'D)
Oh, this is so much better than a wedding.
