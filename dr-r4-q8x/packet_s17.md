## SCENE 17 — INT. CROSS PENTHOUSE — DAMIEN'S OFFICE — NIGHT

*DAMIEN at his desk, a "bug report" form pulled up on a tablet.
JIM hovers, sensing danger.*

DAMIEN
File a report with the vendor.

JIM
About?

DAMIEN
The unit. She's a different build after eight p.m.

JIM
Different how?

DAMIEN
Less statistical. She argued with me about sincerity in weddings.

way that my calendar can't explain.

*A beat. Jim chooses his words like they're explosive.*

JIM
So your complaint is... she's better in the evenings.

DAMIEN
My complaint is she's inconsistent. Document it.

JIM
Documenting. "Unit is less accurate and more pleasant after eight."

DAMIEN
That's not what I— yes. That. Exactly that.

*He stares at the wall. Jim stares at him.*

JIM
Sir. Permission to observe.

DAMIEN
Denied.

JIM
Observing anyway — you're smiling. The actual file-photo people
would weep. Whatever's wrong with the toaster, don't let IT patch it.
