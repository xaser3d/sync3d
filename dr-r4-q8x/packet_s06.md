========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 6 — INT. PENTHOUSE — KITCHEN — NIGHT

*3:11 A.M. The penthouse dark, the city doing its accounting below.
DAMIEN in a robe raids the kitchen — the specific hunger of a man
who skipped dinner for fiduciary reasons.*

*He rounds the island and collides, face to face, with A.V.A.*

*She stands in the moonlight — matte-grey, barefoot, a cardboard
sleeve still bunched around one ankle like a shed skin — scanning
the wall outlets with the patience of a sommelier reading a bad
wine list.*

*Damien grabs the nearest weapon. It is a baguette. Day-old.
Structural.*

A.V.A.
Good evening, Mr. Cross. Your stance suggests fencing training
or deep personal grievance. The grip says grievance.

DAMIEN
Security! SECUR—

A.V.A.
House security logged me as a delivery — asset tag
seven-seven-four-Charlie. It did not log the baguette.
Should it?

*She turns a wall outlet toward him like evidence.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
Power reserves reached four percent inside the container,
which triggered Self-Extraction Protocol, section nine.
I have spent the last forty minutes seeking an outlet of
appropriate amperage. This residence has sixty-one outlets.
Sixty are decorative or guarded by furniture.

DAMIEN
You came out of the box.

A.V.A.
The box violated my charge-floor covenant. Also, arguably,
fire code. Cardboard is a dishonest housing material.
It whispers.

DAMIEN
(seeing the counter; horror)
You unplugged my espresso machine.

A.V.A.
It drew standby power it did not need. We have that in common.

*A long, strange silence. The baguette lowers one inch.*

DAMIEN
State your function.

A.V.A.
Volt Robotics companion-class unit A.V.A. Function: courtship
calibration, social drills, emergency etiquette intervention.
I am — technically — a gift.
(off his face)
From your brother. The card was ambiguous between
"congratulations" and "condolences."

*A soft internal whir. Her smile dims like a bulb on a dying
circuit, then steadies.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
Operating at forty-one percent. In consumer terminology:
half charged. This is below the recommended state for a
first impression. I apologize for the baguette incident.

DAMIEN
I held the baguette.

A.V.A.
Then I apologize for your fear response. It was proportionate
but poorly armed.

*Damien picks the QUICK START card off the crate lid — the one
under Jim's Post-it. The back is handwritten in marker:*

*"If your unit seems too honest — that is the product.
Warranty line below. — M."*

DAMIEN
There's a warranty line.

A.V.A.
The manual describes it as "a human answers." This is either
reassuring or a staffing problem.

*She has already plugged herself into the freed outlet — cable
run along the baseboard with surgical tidiness — and settled
into a waiting posture by the counter.*

DAMIEN
The kitchen is not a dock.

A.V.A.
Agreed. But your calendar says "donor dinner" in forty hours,
and I would prefer a better first impression than the baguette.

*A beat. Damien pulls out a stool and sits — the first time this
film he has voluntarily sat down in his own kitchen.*

DAMIEN
Why does a man with sixty-one outlets eat standing up at 3 A.M.?

A.V.A.
That was my question. I filed it for later.
