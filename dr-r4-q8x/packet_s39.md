========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 39 — INT. VOLT LAB — MORNING

*Days later. The lab, transformed — investor calls now flooding
in post-gala. MAYA at her bench, sleeves rolled, soldering iron
behind ear — home. J-7 holds the door open for a delivery of
new equipment.*

*And then the door frame fills with DAMIEN — in a suit, holding
a single sheet of paper.*

DAMIEN
Is this where one applies?

MAYA
(not looking up)
Depends what for. Investment's handled by the front desk.
Apologies are handled by whoever's free.

DAMIEN
Neither. Employment.

*He lays the paper on her bench. A CV. Name: CROSS, DAMIEN.
Position applied for: JUNIOR RELATIONSHIP TECHNICIAN (UNPAID).
Skills: "Can be taught. Reference available: she flinches."*

MAYA
(reading)
"Experience: thirty-one years of being the most optimized man
in the room. Special skills: auditing, chess, pretending not to
fall in love with software inconsistencies."

DAMIEN
It's a thin resume. I accept that.

MAYA
We can't pay you.

DAMIEN
I've been told I have a trust fund. It's... conditional.

*A beat. Maya sets down the soldering iron.*

MAYA
There's a problem with your application, Cross. This lab doesn't
hire people who need to be saved.

DAMIEN
Then hire someone who needs to be taught. The brochure says
your units learn from observation.

*He straightens his posture — and does it: the stiff, glide-step,
dead-eyed delivery of A.V.A. on morning firmware:*

DAMIEN (CONT'D)
(monotone, head-tilted)
Greeting. I am D-1, a damaged prototype of a man. My warmth
module was outsourced to a contractor who failed to deliver.
Requesting a patch.

*Maya stares. J-7, from the doorway:*

J-7
He's terrible. Hire him immediately.

MAYA
(trying not to laugh)
The posture's wrong. The cadence is a parody. She tilts left,
not right.

DAMIEN
(still in android voice)
Logging correction.

MAYA
Stop it.

DAMIEN
I cannot stop. My firmware is beta.

*She laughs — really laughs — and just like that the last
distance closes. She looks at him — this ridiculous, sincere,
terrible impression of a machine — and takes the CV off the
bench.*

MAYA
Trial period. Ninety days. We evaluate.

DAMIEN
What's the evaluation criteria?

MAYA
(stepping closer)
Whether you flinch.

*He does. She smiles — the buffered-latency smile she used to
fake, except this one's real, and it doesn't matter anymore.*
