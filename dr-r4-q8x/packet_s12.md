========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 12 — INT. PENTHOUSE — DAMIEN'S STUDY — NIGHT

*Late. DAMIEN stands before a window with a notecard — practicing
a toast. MAYA-A.V.A. sits on the couch edge, "monitoring."*

DAMIEN
"To the donors — whose generosity—" no. I did that one.

*He flips the card. Reads silently. Lowers it.*

DAMIEN (CONT'D)
This is absurd. I'm rehearsing sincerity.

MAYA-A.V.A.
Sincerity has a rehearsal mode. Most people skip it — that's why
weddings sound like quarterly reports.

DAMIEN
(turning)
Say something a robot wouldn't.

MAYA-A.V.A.
...Request is vague.

DAMIEN
Your brother model this morning told me my haircut suggested an
earnings beat. You just critiqued the sincerity economy of weddings.
Same firmware, different weather.

*Maya holds very still. The robot smile stays on, a fraction too
long.*

MAYA-A.V.A.
Evening parameters emphasize... empathy weighting. Would you like
the statistical version? I can roll back.

DAMIEN
No.

*He says it fast. Too fast. He puts the card down and sits across
from her — the first time he's voluntarily sat near anyone this
film.*

DAMIEN (CONT'D)
The toast is for my father's memorial dinner. Eight years — the
trustees still hold it. Every year I say the same eleven sentences.
The room thanks me. Nobody has ever once corrected a single word.

MAYA-A.V.A.
Then the problem isn't the toast. It's that you're rehearsing it
for them.

DAMIEN
Who else is there?

MAYA-A.V.A.
(quietly)
Rehearse it for someone who'd correct you.

*A long silence. Somewhere a charger hums in the walls.*

DAMIEN
You'd correct me.

MAYA-A.V.A.
I correct you professionally. It's in my contract — literally,
it's module seven.

DAMIEN
Then module seven: what would you say about a man who built a
fortune so he wouldn't need anyone, and succeeded?

*Maya doesn't answer immediately. The android posture slips —
almost imperceptibly — into something human and careful.*

MAYA-A.V.A.
I'd say he should have built smaller.

*Damien stares at her. Somewhere in the walls, the hum of charging
gets very loud.*

DAMIEN
(standing, abrupt)
Session ends. Dock at midnight.

*He goes. Maya sits alone in the posture of a machine, which is to
say — holding still so nothing shows.*
