========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 3 — INT. CROSS TOWER — DAMIEN'S OFFICE — DAY

*VICTOR CROSS enters like a man who has never met a door he didn't
deserve — 40s, warm smile, warm eyes, and behind both a spreadsheet
only he can see. He carries a lacquered box.*

VICTOR
Brother. Heard about the trust thing. Terrible news. Father's clause —
so antiquated. So... romantic.

DAMIEN
You dug it up yourself, didn't you.

VICTOR
I may have pointed the trustees at the right shelf. Family duty.

*He sets the box down, opens it — a sleek product card: A.V.A.*

VICTOR (CONT'D)
But I'm not just a snake, Damien. I come bearing solutions.

DAMIEN
You're exactly a snake.

VICTOR
A snake that solves. Read the card.

DAMIEN
(reads)
"A.V.A. — Adaptive Virtual Companion. Executive-tier relationship
calibration. Courtship coaching, candidate vetting, emotional
interfacing." Victor. This is a sex toy.

VICTOR
It's a coach. For people like you — people who can negotiate a merger
in four minutes and cause a waitress to cry by ordering coffee.

DAMIEN
That happened once.

VICTOR
Twice. I keep a file. Look — you need to learn courtship in eight weeks.
You can't learn a woman like a term sheet. But you can learn her like
a system. A.V.A. is the only model certified for —

*He checks the card.*

VICTOR (CONT'D)
— "emotional interface at the executive level." She runs drills. She
evaluates your performance. She never files a harassment suit, which,
frankly, on your dating record—

DAMIEN
How much.

VICTOR
More than it's worth. You'll pay it, because it's the only game in
town and you know it. Same as always — I find the thing you need,
I take my commission, you resent me beautifully.

*A beat. Damien looks at the card. At the empty chair across his desk
where the photographer fled. At forty floors of nobody to call.*

DAMIEN
Send the contract.

VICTOR
Already in your inbox. And Damien — genuinely. I hope this works out
for you.

*He smiles as he leaves. It is a smile that has been appraised.*
