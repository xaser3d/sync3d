## SCENE 38 — INT. BALLROOM — TERRACE — NIGHT

*DAMIEN alone on the terrace — the same posture as the rooftop
scene, but the glass is drunk this time. MAYA steps out. He
doesn't turn.*

DAMIEN
The engineer.

MAYA
The billionaire.

DAMIEN
Former. Possibly. The trustees are still arguing about what
"conditional" means.

*A silence. He turns. They're four feet apart and have never been
further — because for the first time there's no costume between
them.*

DAMIEN (CONT'D)
You were the evening build.

MAYA
Every evening. The chess, the toast, the proximity event —
all me. The flinch especially.

DAMIEN
I knew something was wrong in week two. I filed a bug report.
I told Jim she was less accurate and better after eight p.m.
I documented falling in love with a software inconsistency.

MAYA
That's the most romantic thing anyone's ever said to me. It's
also the saddest.

DAMIEN
Maya — the contract made you lie. I'm not going to pretend I
understand what the last two months were, on your side.

MAYA
They were a deployment.

DAMIEN
Your cortisol—

MAYA
(weakly)
Decreases forty percent in your presence, yes, J-7 already
violated that HIPAA.

*He almost smiles — the real one, the one that never makes the
file photo.*

DAMIEN
Then here's the truth without a costume: I don't want the
version of you that's warm mode, or warm anything. I want the
engineer who told me I should have built smaller — when she
was supposed to be telling me my dental symmetry was in the
94th percentile.

MAYA
(a beat)
Your dental symmetry is in the 94th percentile.

DAMIEN
I know. I had it measured after she said it.

*She laughs — the real laugh, the crooked one, no buffering.
And this time, when the moment comes, neither of them calls it
a sanctioned proximity event.*
