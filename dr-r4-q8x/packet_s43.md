========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 43 — EXT./INT. THE WEDDING — DAY (MAYBE-TAG)

*A small wedding — garden, not ballroom. SOPHIA maid of honor;
JIM best man; PENHALIGON beaming in the front row with the
yellowed page in his breast pocket; the trustees present but
disarmed; J-7 ring-bearer, solemn as a crypt.*

*Officiating — in a suit, a stole, and total self-seriousness —
A.V.A.*

A.V.A.
We are gathered to witness a union my models initially scored
at eleven percent. I have since been corrected — repeatedly,
and at length, by both parties.

*Laughter.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
Damien Cross: a man who could optimize everything except the
one thing that mattered. Maya Lind: a woman who could debug
everything except her own heart. Together they form a
statistically anomalous unit — two humans who chose each other
in full knowledge of each other's failure modes, which is the
only definition of love my firmware has been able to retain.

*She pauses — and if a machine could be moved, she is.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
By the power vested in me by the state, the trustees, and a
charge window nobody is currently using — you may commit the
proximity event.

*They kiss. The garden applauds. J-7 quietly extends one
manipulator and hands A.V.A. a tissue she cannot use.*

J-7
For convention.

A.V.A.
Logging.
