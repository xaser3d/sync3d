========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 11 — INT. CROSS PENTHOUSE — TRAINING SALON — EVENING

*A.V.A. — charged, real — stands before DAMIEN with the posture of a
fencing instructor. A wall display reads: COURTSHIP CALIBRATION —
MODULE 1.*

A.V.A.
Module one: compliments. The human compliment is a social checksum —
it verifies attention. Protocol: open with a measurable attribute.
Example: "Your dental symmetry places you in the ninety-fourth
percentile."

DAMIEN
Noted.

A.V.A.
Second example: "Your haircut suggests decisiveness and a quarterly
earnings beat."

JIM (off to the side, into his sleeve)
Oh, she's going to get him killed.

DAMIEN
These are metrics, not compliments. A person would hear the
spreadsheet.

A.V.A.
Clarify the desired emotional payload.

DAMIEN
You're the machine. You clarify it.

*A.V.A. processes. The wall display spins through seventeen
variations, all terrible.*

*That evening — A.V.A. docks. Lights dim. A beat later, MAYA-A.V.A.
glides in — the switch now a routine.*

MAYA-A.V.A.
Resume module one.

DAMIEN
Your earlier build told me to compliment a woman's dental
percentile.

MAYA-A.V.A.
And?

DAMIEN
It's terrible advice.

MAYA-A.V.A.
Yes. A compliment that sounds computed is an audit, not a gift.
Try this instead: ask her what she'd save from a fire.

DAMIEN
That's not a compliment. That's a question.

MAYA-A.V.A.
The best compliment is a question. It says: I believe your answer
will be interesting. Nobody believes that enough.

*Damien looks at her. A long diagnostic look.*

DAMIEN
That's not in your modules.

MAYA-A.V.A.
(a beat too smooth)
Beta plugin.

DAMIEN
Name it.

MAYA-A.V.A.
...Curiosity.dll.

JIM (from the corner, into his coffee)
I'm going to be sick.
