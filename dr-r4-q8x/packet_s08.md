========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 8 — INT. CROSS PENTHOUSE — DAY INTO EVENING (THE DEMO)

*MAYA arrives with a toolbox and a lab coat over street clothes.
She scales the exploded crate like a firefighter into a window
and retrieves the DOCK — a torus of brushed steel — from the
foam.*

DAMIEN
The repair girl.

MAYA
The repair girl is the founder, lead engineer, and tonight
your entire warranty department. Dock first, dignity later.

*A.V.A. steps onto the dock. Light runs up her seams. Maya works
a diagnostics tablet; the graph makes her jaw tighten.*

MAYA (CONT'D)
Battery curve is worse than spec. Social load triples her
draw — she'll do four hours on, six charging, and every
surprise costs minutes she doesn't have.

DAMIEN
Fix it.

MAYA
That's the ten-week repair. You have forty guests in five
hours — and Victor has told three of them a "Cross family
prototype" will demonstrate tonight.

DAMIEN
Victor isn't coming.

JIM
He sent representatives, sir. With cameras. And a term-sheet
folder, which is worse.

*THAT EVENING — the dining room. Eight important people, a
string quartet, wine like property deeds. A.V.A. is seated
beside Damien — flawless posture, charming on rails. MAYA,
in borrowed catering black, hovers at the service door with
her tablet.*

DONOR'S WIFE
You're the first companion model I've met, dear. What do you
think of Damien?

A.V.A.
Mr. Cross exhibits high intelligence, low self-disclosure, and
a cholesterol profile suggesting he negotiates with food as he
does with competitors.

CHEF (passing, quietly)
She means the steak, sir.

*Laughter around the table — uncertain, but laughter. Maya's
tablet flashes red: 23%... 19%... Victor's buyers lean in.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
The marbling in Wagyu correlates to his negotiating style:
expensive, layered, and ultimately—

*Her eyes flicker. A soft internal whir. Her smile dims like a
bulb on a dying circuit.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
Power reserves at three percent. Initiating — meditation —
mode—

*She freezes mid-syllable, perfectly upright, pleasantly smiling
at nothing. The table applauds uncertainly.*

DONOR
Is she—?

JIM
Meditating. She meditates. A lot. Very spiritual machine.

*Across the table, a buyer raises a phone and photographs the
frozen android. MAYA is already moving for the service
corridor, tablet first.*
