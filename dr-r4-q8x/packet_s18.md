========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 18 — INT. CROSS TOWER — STRATEGY MEETING — DAY

*A.V.A. — real, charged — sits in the corner of a strategy meeting,
"observing." Twelve executives around a table; a mole hunt in
progress — confidential terms keep leaking to a rival.*

CFO
The leak has to be in this room. We've rotated passwords twice.

GENERAL COUNSEL
Polygraph everyone. I mean it.

A.V.A.
Unnecessary.

*Every head turns.*

A.V.A. (CONT'D)
Mr. Whitfield has expensed forty-one lunches "with vendor." No
vendor orders dessert alone twice a week. His expense lunches
align with the publication dates of the leaks within a two-day
window. The probability of coincidence is 0.3 percent.

*All eyes to WHITFIELD, a damp man in an expensive suit.*

WHITFIELD
That's — I take clients to lunch!

A.V.A.
You expensed a lunch on the day of your mother's funeral. Either
the client was family, or the lunch was.

*Whitfield is escorted out. The room sits in appalled silence.
DAMIEN, at the head of the table, makes a note:*

DAMIEN
Add her to the audit committee.

JIM (aside, to no one)
She's a toaster on the audit committee.
