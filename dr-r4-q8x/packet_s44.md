## SCENE 44 — EPILOGUE CARDS (ROLLING)

*— Cross Holdings posts its best year in a decade. Analysts
credit "leadership stability." Damien credits the 8pm build.*

*— Volt Robotics ships the A-500: the first companion unit sold
with a disclosure page and a union card.*

*— Sophia Vane runs investor relations and is, by unanimous
vote, the scariest person in the building.*

*— Victor Cross writes a memoir: "How I Almost Owned Everything."

for the shelf where things that tried to break her get displayed.*

"He who waits for parts loses his patina."*

of the counter, for the unit who is always, technically,
meditating.*

**THE END**
