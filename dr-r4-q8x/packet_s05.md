========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 5 — INT. CROSS PENTHOUSE — GRAND FOYER — DAY (DELIVERY DAY)

*A penthouse like a bank vault that learned interior design. Two
couriers wrestle a refrigerator-sized cardboard crate through the
doors — VOLT ROBOTICS stenciled on the side, THIS SIDE UP printed
beside an arrow that currently points sideways.*

*DAMIEN crosses the marble on a phone — mid-crisis, board business.
He signs the courier's tablet without looking at it once.*

COURIER
Where do you want the, uh — unit, sir?

DAMIEN
(into the phone)
Put it with the others.

*JIM — his aide, 27, who understands everything and approves of
nothing — steers the crate toward THE ARCHIVE OF GOOD INTENTIONS:
an alcove of unopened deliveries. A wine crate. Something
Peloton-shaped. An award still in shrink-wrap.*

JIM
This one says FRAGILE in six languages.

DAMIEN
(still on the phone)
Then it will fit in beautifully.

*The crate goes in beside its brethren. Jim peels the QUICK START /
WARRANTY card out of the packing sleeve and — out of professional
conscience — sticks it on top under a Post-it: "OPEN ME. 30-DAY
RETURN."*

*DAYS PASS — a comedy of neglect:*

*— The crate gains a lamp. Then a stack of architectural digests.
Then somebody's gym bag.*

*— At 2 A.M. it emits a soft ventilation hum. The house manager
files a ticket: "radiator again."*

*— Once, muffled, from inside: a polite chime. Jim unplugs the
smart speaker in reprisal. The chime was not the smart speaker.*

*— A florist uses it as a stepladder. It holds. Politely.*

*Then — VICTOR on the penthouse wallscreen, smiling like a man who
has already spent the money.*

VICTOR
Did my present arrive?

DAMIEN
What present.

*Victor's eyes track past him — to the alcove. To the lamp on the
crate. His smile achieves new orbit.*

VICTOR
Keep the wrapping till the weekend. She's better with ceremony.

*He's gone. Damien stares at the alcove. The crate hums. Faintly.
Contentedly.*

DAMIEN
Jim.

JIM
Already ordered a crowbar, sir.
