========== SCREENPLAY SCENE ==========

## SCENE 26 — INT. VICTOR'S OFFICE — DAY

*VICTOR, VEGA (a fixer in a very good coat), and a tablet of
tabloid mockups. On screen: photos of Damien and A.V.A. at events.*

VEGA
Your brother's companion is flawless. Too flawless. Either the
robot is the best product ever shipped — or something's wrong
with the robot.

VICTOR
Something's wrong with the robot. I've suspected since week two —
Damien smiles now. Damien doesn't smile; the file photo people
have a standing order.

VEGA
What do you want me to find?

VICTOR
The seams. Battery schedules, vendor NDA, whoever services the
unit — and the candidate situation. If he hasn't proposed to
anyone by the gala, the clause drops him into my lap anyway.
But I'd rather hand the trustees a fraud. Fraud is forever;
a bachelor clause is just paperwork.

*He slides a photo across — Sophia Vane.*

VICTOR (CONT'D)
And resurrect plan B. The payroll socialite. Get her back in the
game — I don't care what it costs. If Damien won't court, the
courtship gets manufactured for him.
